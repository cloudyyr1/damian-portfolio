Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026.

This website (the "Site") is operated by Gabriel Damian Radu, an independent freelancer based in Larnaka, Cyprus ("I", "me", "my"). This policy explains what information is collected when you visit or interact with the Site, why, and what rights you have over it. It's written to meet the general standards of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and reflects good-practice principles recognized in most other privacy laws worldwide. It is not a substitute for legal advice, and if your local law grants you rights beyond what's listed here, those rights are honored as well.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Gabriel Damian Radu is the data controller for any personal information collected through this Site. Contact for any privacy-related question or request: damian@malaka.cy.

2. What this Site does not do

This Site does not use cookies, does not use analytics or tracking scripts (like Google Analytics or Meta Pixel), does not use browser local storage to track you, does not run advertising of any kind, and does not automatically collect or store any personal data simply because you visited a page.

3. What data is collected, and why

3.1 Information you choose to give me

If you contact me by email, Instagram, or phone using the details on this Site, I will have whatever information you choose to share — typically your name, email address, phone number, and details about your business or project. This is used only to respond to you and, if we work together, to deliver and invoice the agreed services. It is not shared with third parties, sold, or used for marketing without your separate consent.

Legal basis: this processing is based on your consent (you contacted me voluntarily) and, where we proceed with a project, on the performance of a contract between us.

3.2 Technical data from loading the page

This Site loads typefaces from Google Fonts' servers. Loading a font this way sends your device's IP address and browser information to Google, which may process that data outside your country (including the United States), under Google's own privacy policy and applicable data transfer safeguards (such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses). I do not control or receive this data myself — Google does not report any of it back to me. If you'd prefer this not to happen at all, you can use browser extensions that block third-party font requests, though this may affect how the Site looks.

Standard web server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) may be automatically generated by GitHub Pages, the hosting provider for this Site, as part of normal web hosting operation. I do not access or use this data personally; it is governed by GitHub's own privacy statement.

4. Cookies

This Site does not set any cookies of its own. No consent banner is shown because there is nothing on this Site that requires cookie consent under the EU ePrivacy Directive (PECR) or similar laws — only essential, cookie-free page loading occurs.

5. How long information is kept

Information you send me directly (like an email) is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry or complete any agreed work, plus a reasonable period afterward for accounting, legal, or tax record-keeping purposes (typically no longer than required under Cyprus and EU record-keeping obligations).

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: access the personal data I hold about you; correct inaccurate data; request deletion of your data; restrict or object to processing; request a copy of your data in a portable format; and withdraw consent at any time (which does not affect processing already carried out). California residents also have the right to know what categories of personal information are collected and to request deletion — I do not sell personal information, and none is shared for cross-context behavioral advertising.

To exercise any of these rights, email damian@malaka.cy. I will respond within a reasonable time and, in any case, within the timeframes required by applicable law (for example, one month under GDPR).

If you believe your data has been mishandled, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority — for Cyprus, this is the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection.

7. Children's privacy

This Site is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 16. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me and it will be deleted.

8. Third-party links

This Site links out to Instagram and to your device's email and phone apps. Those services have their own privacy policies and practices, which I don't control and this policy doesn't cover.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes to the Site or legal requirements. The "last updated" date at the top will always reflect the latest version.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to damian@malaka.cy.
